A point of view

There have never been more reasons to react. It has never been more valuable not to.

AI that no longer just executes but decides, geopolitical instability, economic uncertainty, an increasingly unpredictable climate — no single one of these forces defines leadership for the years ahead. What does define it is whether, facing all of them at once, a leader holds a clear head and their own judgment, or passes on to their team the same anxiety they’re feeling. It’s the conversation I work through with every client.
