Cookie Policy

This website uses only technical cookies and local storage necessary for it to function. We do not use advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics cookies.

What we store and why

Language preference (md_lang): remembers whether you prefer to view the site in Spanish or English.
Cookie consent (md_cookie_consent): remembers your choice on the cookie banner so it isn’t shown again.

Both are stored via the browser’s localStorage, not third-party cookies, and are never shared with anyone.

Forms and providers

When you submit a contact form or the Executive Impact Snapshot, the data is processed through Netlify (hosting) and, if you request your result by email, through Resend (email delivery). Neither of these services installs advertising cookies in your browser. More detail in our Privacy Policy.

How to disable them

You can clear local storage at any time from your browser’s privacy settings. Since these are technical cookies, disabling them may affect features such as remembering your preferred language.
