Leadership Circle Profile®

The leadership assessment that connects your actions to your patterns of thought

The Leadership Circle Profile® (LCP) is one of the most widely used 360° leadership assessments at senior executive level internationally — the only one that reveals, in a single report, both the behaviors that generate your impact and the internal beliefs that drive them.

How it works

Two domains, one map

Original illustration by Mauro Delgado, inspired by the distinction between reactive and creative patterns.

Creative competencies

They measure how you achieve results, bring out the best in others, lead with vision, act with integrity, and improve your organization’s systems. These are the behaviors that build trust and sustainable results over time.

Reactive tendencies

Leadership styles that prioritize caution over results, self-protection over productive engagement, or control over real alignment. They surface when a leader’s sense of security depends on approval, control, or distance.

The LCP is the only instrument that measures both domains at once and integrates them into a single report — making it possible to see, at a glance, what’s working, what isn’t, and why.

Why it matters

More than a personality diagnostic

The LCP includes a Leadership Effectiveness measure directly correlated with business outcomes, plus an index showing the real balance between your creative and reactive patterns — and between attending to people and attending to task. It’s the most complete picture of how your leadership translates, day to day, into real impact.

How we work with your LCP

Two paths, depending on what you need

Standalone assessment

You receive your full 360° report and a debrief session with me to interpret it in depth — an objective starting point, with no need to begin a coaching engagement.

Integrated into your coaching

The LCP becomes the objective starting point of a full executive coaching engagement, and the rest of the work is built on it.
