Giving back

For the past two years I’ve put my strategy and scaling experience at the service of the TRAM Barcelona Open, Barcelona’s wheelchair tennis tournament, as my way of giving back some of what I’ve received. I met its founder and his story inspired me to help him grow his dream. None of that is thanks to me — the credit belongs to its team and its players — but I keep collaborating because I believe in what it represents: real inclusion, not just sport.
