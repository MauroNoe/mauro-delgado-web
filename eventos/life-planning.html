Who it’s for

For professionals who want this year to be different

The year goes by and the goals stay in January

You start with good intentions, but the day-to-day takes over. You need a plan that survives February.

Your career moves forward, but other areas wait

Health, relationships, learning, time for yourself: what matters but is never urgent. Here it carries the same weight as work.

You want method, not just motivation

The motivation from an event lasts days. A plan with clear reasons, concrete actions and daily habits lasts all year.

What you take away

You leave with your year designed, not a page of notes

  • An honest review of your year: achievements, lessons and what you don’t want to repeat.
  • Your one- and three-year vision, and the reasons that will keep you going when motivation drops.
  • Your Life Plan 2027 in three areas (personal growth, career and life), with three goals in each, their «why» and three committed actions.
  • A diagnosis of how you interpret challenge, mistakes and change.
  • Your daily rituals and habits to sustain the plan.
  • The Life Plan workbook, to review your progress throughout the year.
The programme

Four modules, two full days

01

Mindset

How you interpret challenge, mistakes and change, with a mindset test based on Carol Dweck’s research, and which patterns move you towards or away from what you want.

02

Closing the year and lessons learned

Before planning, closing: the year’s moments and achievements, what you don’t want to repeat, and how you turned seemingly distant dreams into reality.

03

Your Life Plan 2027

Your one- and three-year vision, the «why» behind each goal and your roadmap in three areas, with concrete actions from day one.

04

Habits and rituals

The daily systems that sustain the plan when motivation drops: mental preparation, focus and progress reviews.

An original framework

The seasons of life

Life doesn’t move in a straight line, but in cycles. Recognising which season you’re in changes which goals make sense this year, and removes the pressure to always grow at the same pace.

Spring

Prepare and sow

Summer

Grow and expand

Autumn

Evaluate and let go

Winter

Rest and redefine

The approach

Achievement and fulfilment

Life Planning combines the science of achieving goals with the art of enjoying the journey. It draws on research into growth mindset (Carol Dweck), learning and rethinking (Adam Grant) and habit formation, and on 20+ years leading teams and 6,000 hours of executive coaching. I run it every January, with small groups so everyone has space.

Frequently asked questions

Is it only for executives?

No. Life Planning is designed for professionals in general who want to plan the year with method across every area of their life, not just their career.

What language is it in?

Spanish. The workshop is delivered in person and in Spanish.

What does the price include?

Both full working days, 9:00 to 18:00, the Life Plan workbook and all materials. The price is €495 plus VAT. Accommodation, meals and transport are not included.

Where does it take place?

In Mataró (Barcelona). The planned venue is Hotel Atenea Port, pending confirmation. I’ll confirm the exact location when you reserve.

What happens if the minimum number of participants isn’t reached?

Life Planning runs with a minimum of 8 people, so the group dynamic works. If that number isn’t reached, I’ll let you know in advance and the workshop will be cancelled. If you had already made any payment, it would be refunded in full.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Just block out both full days and come ready to step away from the day-to-day and think about yourself.
